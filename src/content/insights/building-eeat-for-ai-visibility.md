Google introduced E-A-T (Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) in its Search Quality Rater Guidelines years ago. In 2022, Google added a fourth pillar — Experience — making it E-E-A-T. While originally designed to guide human quality raters, these principles have become equally critical for AI platform visibility.

AI models, like Google's algorithms, need signals to determine which sources are credible enough to cite. E-E-A-T provides that framework.

## Breaking Down E-E-A-T

### Experience

Demonstrates that the content creator has first-hand, real-world experience with the topic. For a restaurant review site, this means actually visiting restaurants. For an SEO advisory, it means sharing results from real client campaigns.

**How to demonstrate Experience:**
- Include case studies with specific, measurable outcomes
- Share personal anecdotes and lessons learned
- Use first-person language where appropriate ("In our work with over 50 Hong Kong businesses, we've found...")
- Publish before-and-after comparisons with real data

### Expertise

Shows that the content creator has deep knowledge and qualifications in the subject area. This is about credentials, depth of coverage, and accuracy.

**How to demonstrate Expertise:**
- Display author credentials, certifications, and professional backgrounds
- Create comprehensive, in-depth content that covers topics thoroughly
- Cite primary sources and peer-reviewed research
- Maintain an active presence in professional communities and publications

### Authoritativeness

Reflects the reputation of the content creator or website within its industry. Authority is largely built externally — through recognition by others.

**How to demonstrate Authoritativeness:**
- Earn mentions in industry publications and news outlets
- Collect and showcase client testimonials and reviews
- Speak at conferences and contribute to industry forums
- Build a consistent brand presence across multiple reputable platforms
- Maintain an accurate and comprehensive Google Knowledge Panel

### Trustworthiness

Encompasses the overall reliability of the website — accurate information, transparent business practices, secure browsing, and clear contact information.

**How to demonstrate Trustworthiness:**
- Use HTTPS and maintain strong site security
- Provide clear contact information, privacy policies, and terms of service
- Ensure factual accuracy with regular content audits
- Display trust badges, certifications, and affiliations
- Respond to reviews and customer feedback publicly

## E-E-A-T Tactics for AI Platform Citation

AI models evaluate sources similarly to how Google's quality raters do — looking for signals that a source is credible, knowledgeable, and reliable. Here's how to translate E-E-A-T into GEO results:

### 1. Author Entity Optimization

Create dedicated author pages for each content contributor, including:
- Professional biography and headshot
- Credentials and certifications
- Links to published work and social profiles
- Schema markup (Person schema with `sameAs` properties)

### 2. Content Depth Over Breadth

AI models prefer citing a comprehensive 3,000-word guide over ten thin 300-word posts. Invest in pillar content that thoroughly covers core topics in your industry.

### 3. Third-Party Validation

The most powerful E-E-A-T signal for AI platforms is external recognition:
- Guest posts on authoritative industry sites
- Press coverage in relevant publications
- Inclusion in industry directories and roundups
- Positive reviews on Google, industry platforms, and social media

### 4. Structured Knowledge Presentation

Present your expertise in formats AI models can easily parse:
- FAQ pages with clear question-answer pairs
- Glossaries defining industry terms
- Step-by-step how-to guides with numbered instructions
- Comparison tables and data visualizations with alt text

### 5. Consistent Entity Information

Ensure your business name, description, founding date, location, and key personnel are consistent across:
- Your website
- Google Business Profile
- Social media profiles
- Industry directories
- Wikipedia (if applicable)

Inconsistencies create entity confusion that reduces AI citation confidence.

## Measuring Your E-E-A-T Progress

Track these indicators to gauge improvement:

| Signal | How to Measure |
|--------|---------------|
| Experience | Case study publication rate, client outcome data |
| Expertise | Content depth scores, author credential completeness |
| Authoritativeness | Brand mention volume, press coverage, review ratings |
| Trustworthiness | Site security score, content accuracy audits, review response rate |

For AI-specific measurement, regularly query your target topics across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Note whether your brand is cited, how it's described, and which competitors appear instead.

## A 90-Day E-E-A-T Action Plan

**Days 1–30: Audit and Foundation**
- Audit existing content for E-E-A-T gaps
- Create or update author pages with full credentials
- Implement Organization and Person schema markup
- Ensure NAP consistency across all platforms

**Days 31–60: Content and Authority Building**
- Publish 2–3 in-depth, experience-driven case studies
- Launch an FAQ hub covering your top 20 customer questions
- Begin outreach for guest posts and industry mentions
- Update top-performing pages with fresh data and insights

**Days 61–90: Measurement and Iteration**
- Run AI visibility audits across target platforms
- Analyze which content types earn the most citations
- Refine content strategy based on AI citation patterns
- Establish ongoing monitoring and reporting cadence

## Conclusion

E-E-A-T isn't a checkbox exercise — it's an ongoing commitment to demonstrating that your brand is the most credible source in your space. For AI platform visibility, this commitment directly translates into citation frequency and recommendation likelihood. Start building your E-E-A-T foundation today, and both your Google rankings and AI presence will strengthen together.
